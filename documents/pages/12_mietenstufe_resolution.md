# What the Mietstufe leaves open

<img src="/kdu_vs_wohngeld/mietenstufe_dispersion.png" style="max-height: 330px; margin: 0 auto;" />

Knowing only a Gemeinde's Mietstufe leaves its cap uncertain by about **± 83 €** a month.
The Wohngeld-based Proxy tracks the local caps in the cheap classes and sits **40–50 €**
above them in the expensive ones.
