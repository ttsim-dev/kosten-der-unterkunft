# How important is this in reality? I don't know.

- **BA Wohnkostenstatistik**, single Bedarfsgemeinschaften, **402 Jobcenter**.

- Single Bedarfsgemeinschaften pay **446 €** of cold rent a month. Jobcenter recognise
  **430 €**.

- That is a difference in **euros, not in Bedarfsgemeinschaften** — equally consistent
  with 90 % losing nothing and 10 % losing a great deal.

- The extent to which the ceiling **binds at the household level is unknown**.
