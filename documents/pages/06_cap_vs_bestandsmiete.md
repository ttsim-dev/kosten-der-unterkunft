# Where the ceiling sits in the local market

<img src="/market_rent_comparison/cap_over_bestandsmiete.png" style="max-height: 400px; margin: 0.2rem auto;" />

The median Gemeinde sets its cap at **1.29 times** the average local rent; it falls below
that average in **6 %** of Gemeinden.
