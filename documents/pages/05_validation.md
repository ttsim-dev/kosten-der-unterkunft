# Do the collected caps match what Jobcenter recognise?

The **BA Wohnkostenstatistik** publishes, per Jobcenter, the mean Bruttokaltmiete that
Bedarfsgemeinschaften pay and the mean amount recognised. Single Bedarfsgemeinschaften,
**354 Jobcenter**:

| predicts the recognised Bruttokaltmiete | correlation |
|:------------------------------------------|------------:|
| **Angemessene KdU** (collected)            |    **0.90** |
| Wohngeld-based Proxy                       |        0.77 |

<br/>

In **335 of 354** Jobcenter the mean recognised amount sits **below** the collected cap —
as it must, if the cap was read correctly.
