# The problem

- **Kosten der Unterkunft** are a major part of what the Grundsicherung pays out under
  SGB II and SGB XII.

- **§ 22 SGB II** caps them at what is *angemessen* — and never defines it.

- Roughly **400 Träger** each publish their own **Richtlinie**, and the resulting caps
  vary at **Gemeinde** level.

- To my knowledge no complete collection of these thresholds exists, though they
  enter the Bedarf of every simulated household.

<br/>

One identical single-person household, moved across Germany:
**minimum 272.64 € · median 430.50 € · maximum 911.00 €** per month.
