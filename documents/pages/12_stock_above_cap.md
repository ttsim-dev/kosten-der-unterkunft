# What each ceiling prices out of reach

<img src="/market_rent_comparison/share_of_stock_above_cap.png" style="max-height: 270px; margin: 0.2rem auto;" />

- Median Gemeinde: **14.3 %** of the rented stock sits above the **local cap**,
  **12.3 %** above the **Grenze ohne schlüssiges Konzept**.

- Across Gemeinden those per-Gemeinde differences cancel: the aggregate moves **1 point**.

- Both shares use Zensus 2022 **Bestandsmieten**, below asking rents, so each is a lower
  bound.
